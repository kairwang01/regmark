Usual cause and fix. The feed file was generated longer ago than the maxAge configured for it, so whatever it says about price and stock is that old. Check the feed exporter or its scheduled job, and make sure the feed URL serves the latest export rather than a cached copy.

variant.missingA surface lists some of a product's variants and leaves others out.

Usual cause and fix. The structured data describes only the default variant. Emit one Offer per variant, as a ProductGroup with hasVariant or as an offers array, each with its own sku, price and availability. In an agent feed, send one row per variant, out of stock or not, and hold a variant back with is_eligible_search=false rather than by leaving it out.
